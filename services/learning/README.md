# Learning Service

Queue-driven, idempotent workflows for:

- upload and connector ingestion;
- transcription and document extraction;
- cleanup, chunking and editable learning-unit generation;
- embeddings, retrieval and reindexing;
- diagram extraction, recreation and human curation;
- student memory, clustering, confusion and opportunity intelligence.

Every job is tenant-scoped, resumable, costed and auditable. Named providers are adapters selected by capability routers.
